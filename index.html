Cognitive Load Theory (excerpt)

Working memory is finite. When intrinsic load (task difficulty) and extraneous load (poor environment) exceed capacity, performance collapses — often invisibly to the person experiencing it.

Behavioral signals leak this overload long before self-report does: increased deletion rates, context-switching, and motor micro-stuttering are well-documented correlates of fatigue and stress.

The goal of this workspace is not to test you — it is to observe you, the way a profiler observes a running program: non-invasively, continuously, and honestly.

Scroll & read naturally — idle time and scroll behavior feed the engine too.
